Method & assumptions. Account $100,000; flat risk $100 per trade = 1R (dollars = R × $100, independent of stop size). Wins = trades closed in profit, losses = trades closed in loss. Streaks are consecutive wins / losses in date order. First qualifying high-volume breakout per day. Dealing costs are modelled as one bid/ask spread per trade (US30 2.0 pts, NAS100 1.5 pts; spread-bet indices carry no separate commission) — toggle Gross / After costs above. Backtest, not live results; slippage on stops not modelled; 2023 and 2026 are part-years. Figures reproducible from scripts/dollar_report.py.
