03Dollar breakdown — selected scheme

Scheme

Method. Step trail: at each k×(step) of favourable movement the stop ratchets to (k−1)×(step) — so a 1.0R step means breakeven at +1R, +1R at +2R, and so on. Breakeven@1R moves the stop to entry once +1R is reached, then holds. Bar-by-bar M5 simulation with a pessimistic intrabar rule (a bar is never allowed to raise its own stop, and a bar spanning both stop and target is counted as a stop) — results are conservative. 1R = $100; dollars net of dealing spread. Backtest on 3 yrs of cTrader M5, not live. Source: scripts/trail_study.py.
