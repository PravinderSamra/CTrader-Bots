Daily Systems bot

Two "buy the dip in an uptrend" strategies, run separately on US500, US100, US30, GER40 and gold. Buy trades only.

A. Pavel's mean reversion

Buy at the daily close when all three are true:

  1. The close is below its 5-day average.
  2. The drop below that average is unusually large: more than 2.5 times the average gap of the last 20 days.
  3. The close is above its 200-day average (long-term uptrend).

Sell at the first daily close back above the 5-day average, or after 4 days, whichever comes first.

D. Naoufel's RSI(2)

RSI(2) scores the last two days' moves from 0 to 100. Below 20 means a sharp two-day drop.

Buy when RSI(2) is below 20 and the close is above its 200-day average.

Sell when RSI(2) rises above 70 (the bounce has happened), usually 2-5 days later.

Stop loss and take profit

  • No fixed take profit. The sell rules do that job: each trade closes once the bounce has happened.
  • Emergency stop loss on every trade: 3 x the average daily range of the last 20 days below the entry, usually 4-6% away. A safety net, not the normal way out.

Size: "Notional per position" = $13,000

Notional is the value of the position, not the amount at risk. $13,000 moves about $130 for every 1% the market moves (US500 at 6,500 = 2 units). At this size: average win about +$160 to +$220, average loss about -$270 to -$300, emergency stop about -$500 to -$800.

Timing and safety

  • Decisions use the daily close; orders go in about 11 pm UK time (one hour after FTMO's new daily bar opens).
  • Daily guard: down $2,000 on the day = close everything, stop until tomorrow.
  • Size cap: all open positions together at most 1.5 x the balance.
  • Flat over the weekend: close all Friday 15:30 New York time, reopen Monday with the same size and stop (unless the exit rule fired on Friday). Removes the risk of a big Monday gap the daily guard can't stop.

Backtest results ($100k account, $13k per position)

  • Jan 2024 - Oct 2026, final settings on real tick data: +$15,003, 73% of trades won, worst drawdown 5.25%, worst day -$2,009 (daily guard), every year positive (2024 +$3.5k, 2025 +$5.5k, 2026 to Oct +$6.0k).
  • Settings: A and D on, Notional 13,000, Daily Guard 2,000, Flat Over Weekend = Yes.
  • Off-switch if live: back to demo if the win rate over the last 50 trades drops below 62%, or drawdown passes ~7%.
