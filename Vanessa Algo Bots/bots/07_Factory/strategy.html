Why it should work

When a stock index has an unusually sharp fall, forced and emotional selling tends to overshoot, and the price usually recovers part of the drop within about a week. Large funds can't easily trade this across 9 smaller index CFDs at once — at our size we can.

The rules

  • Markets: 9 stock indices — US500, US100, US30, GER40, UK100, FRA40, EU50, AUS200, HK50.
  • Buy when an index closes below its lower Bollinger band (the 20-day average minus 2.5 times the 20-day standard deviation). The order is sent 60 minutes after the next daily open, once that market is trading.
  • Sell 5 trading days later. No take profit.
  • Flat over the weekend: every position is closed on Friday at 15:30 New York time (20:30 UK; a market that shuts earlier, such as HK50, closes in its last 45 minutes) and reopened at the Monday order time with the same size and stop. If the 5-day exit falls on Friday's close, it simply stays closed. This avoids Monday gaps and FTMO's triple Friday swap.
  • No normal stop. Testing showed a normal stop sells just before the bounce and wipes out the edge.
  • Disaster stop 10 times the average daily range below the entry, purely for a true crash. It has almost never been hit.
  • One position per index; several indices can be held at once, because sharp falls often hit several together.
  • Risk budget (v1.1): all open positions together never add up to more than 6 full-size positions. If 6 are already open, new ones get a smaller size. So even if every disaster stop hit on the same day, the most it could lose is 30R ($3,000 at half size) — the worst real day in 16 years was 5R.

Size

  • Risk Per R = $200, where R = twice the index's average daily range. The bot works out the position size itself.
  • A normal losing trade costs about $200–400. The worst single trade in 16 years lost about $1,000 (5R); the worst single day was about −$1,100 (5.6R).
  • First 30 live trades at half size ($100 per R), as our Quant Process says.
  • Worst case at once: about $35k of positions, roughly $2.3k of margin at FTMO's 1:15 leverage.

Live settings

  • Attach to a US500.cash, Daily chart; the bot trades all 9 indices itself.
  • Risk Per R = 100 (half size); Flat Over Weekend = Yes; Label = BOLL; everything else at its default.
  • Crash-safe: the bot saves its to-do list every minute and reloads it after a restart. After restarting cTrader, look for the R| line in the log. Avoid restarting between about 23:00 and 00:30 UK, when orders are sent.
  • Log lines: O| opened, C| closed by the 5-day rule, W| weekend close or reopen, X| skipped.

Switch-off rule (monthly review)

Pause the bot if its last 50 trades average below −0.05R, or if its drawdown passes $3,500 (1.5 times the worst the backtest showed). A paused bot is re-tested before it restarts.

After 30 live trades that pass the monthly review, Risk Per R can go up to $200.
