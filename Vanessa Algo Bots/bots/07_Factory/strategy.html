Why it should work

When a stock index has an unusually sharp fall, forced and emotional selling tends to overshoot, and the price usually recovers part of the drop within about a week. Large funds can't easily trade this across 10 smaller index CFDs at once — at our size we can.

The rules

  • Markets: 10 stock indices — US500, US100, US30, US2000, GER40, UK100, FRA40, EU50, AUS200, HK50.
  • Buy when an index closes below its lower Bollinger band (the 20-day average minus 2.5 times the 20-day standard deviation). The order is sent 60 minutes after the next daily open, once that market is trading.
  • Sell 5 trading days later. No take profit.
  • No normal stop. Testing showed a normal stop sells just before the bounce and wipes out the edge.
  • Disaster stop 10 times the average daily range below the entry, purely for a true crash. It has almost never been hit.
  • One position per index; several indices can be held at once, because sharp falls often hit several together.

Size

  • Risk Per R = $200, where R = twice the index's average daily range. The bot works out the position size itself.
  • A normal losing trade costs about $200–400. The worst single trade in 16 years lost about $1,000 (5R); the worst single day was about −$1,100 (5.6R).
  • First 30 live trades at half size ($100 per R), as our Quant Process says.

Switch-off rule (monthly review)

Pause the bot if its last 50 trades average below −0.05R, or if its drawdown passes $3,500 (1.5 times the worst the backtest showed). A paused bot is re-tested before it restarts.
