# 10 — "Training The Eyes" Pattern Library

A concrete, named pattern library distilled from the Inter Equity Trading (IET) "Training The Eyes" series and the recurring recognition drills in every other IET breakdown — usable as a labelled recognition checklist for a human or an AI/bot detector. The series' stated purpose: "putting all of these concepts together and training those eyes to see the system in live time over and over and over... the only way you can do that is by training the eyes" (IET 2026-01-31 "Training The Eyes 12" [00:42]–[00:57]).

Citation format: `(IET YYYY-MM-DD "short title" [MM:SS])` → `../01-transcripts/inter-equity-channel/`. Timestamps are video time from the inline `<hh:mm:ss>` caption tags (the raw files' `[MM:SS]` line prefixes are unreliable — see file 09 §0). Read files 01–03 first for the underlying theory; this file is the recognition layer only.

**Standing caveat from the source:** the author repeatedly warns these are *logic-anchored reads, not shapes to template-match* — "it's not a pattern, it's logic" (IET 2026-06-30 "Live Gold $9k" [07:04]); "we're not trading patterns, we're looking for logic" (IET 2026-04-21 "Masterclass 3" [05:43]). Every entry below therefore states the *why* (whose orders are where) alongside the *what*.

## 0. How to use this library

- Work top-down: classify the higher-timeframe state first (P10–P12 context patterns), then look for the setup patterns (P1–P9) on the execution timeframe. "If you don't see anything on the higher, anything relevant, then you shouldn't be going to the lower time frame" (IET 2026-07-21 "Correct Liquidity" [01:06]).
- Each pattern lists: **Looks like** (geometry to recognize), **Means** (order-flow story), **Confirmed by**, **Invalidated / not it when**, **Action**, **Citations**.
- Training regime the series prescribes: replay-tool drills on historical charts, marking every instance before revealing the outcome — "back test your system repeatedly to train your eyes. Without this, you will struggle to see setups in the live market" (IET 2026-02-03 "Profitable 2026" [02:07]–[02:47]).

## 1. Pattern entries

### P1 — Respected low / respected high (confirmed pool)

- **Looks like:** a low (high) whose wick terminates at/into a prior low (high) or marked area to the left *without closing through it*, followed by a move away. "We got a reaction out of this low. Look to the left-hand side. Where did the reaction come from? We respected this low and went long" (IET 2026-01-31 "Training The Eyes 12" [01:29]–[01:36]).
- **Means:** orders were defended there → resting liquidity now sits beyond the level. "This low has respected this low. Price has communicated to us this is going to be a future level of liquidity. Use it to your advantage" (IET 2026-01-31 [01:46]–[01:52]).
- **Confirmed by:** the move-away; repeated respects strengthen it ("we tapped into this area once, twice, and that fueled this sell-off... respected this high multiple times — once, twice, three times, four times — that tells me we have left a significant amount of liquidity above this high", IET 2026-04-21 "Masterclass 3" [01:42]–[02:02]).
- **Invalidated / not it when:** no touch/tap relationship exists to the left — "if this high never touched into this high and we just had a sell-off... then you wouldn't be plotting a line on this high" (IET 2026-07-21 "Correct Liquidity" [04:46]–[05:06]); or price closes through the level (that is a sweep, not a respect).
- **Action:** plot a line/ray at the level, drag it right; it is a future target or a future arming level ("we understand it's going to be used as maybe a target or we sell after it's taken", IET 2026-01-31 [04:08]–[04:12]). Equal/relative-equal clusters get one level ("look at those equals the market left for us. Definitely a level where price can visit", IET 2026-04-28 "Ping Pong" [03:02]).
- **Citations:** also IET 2026-02-24 [01:02]–[01:22] (daily low respected → "provides me a valid target to sell into"); IET 2026-07-07 [01:23]–[01:43] (daily May-19 low respecting May-8 level); IET 2026-07-14 [01:13] (daily low rejecting swing low).

### P2 — Inducement leg (the BOS sweep of an internal level)

- **Looks like:** a displacement leg that trades through an *internal* high/low in the direction of the current move — retail's "break of structure". On the chart: momentum candles clearing a marked internal level, often into/away from a red-box zone.
- **Means:** participants are being pulled in on the break; their stops now stack beyond the pullback swing. "Look at this internal liquidity point we took out. This induces buyers... Here we have a BOS. So this again induces, entices, whatever you want to call it. Buyers want to step into this market" (IET 2026-01-31 [02:04]–[02:16]); "every single time we make a new high... we are inducing buyers after taking out previous highs... think about this from a psychology perspective... the market sees that as momentum" (IET 2026-06-09 "Everything Liquidity" [01:04]–[01:44]).
- **Confirmed by:** the pullback after the break respecting a level (the induced entries defending), leaving a fresh confirmed pool (P1) behind them (IET 2026-06-09 [03:04]–[03:44]).
- **Invalidated / not it when:** the break happens with no prior confirmed level and no participants to trap (nothing to the left) — then it is just displacement, possibly a void.
- **Action:** never trade the leg itself — "this is not moves we want to be involved in... However, you want to see it occur because this is now a confluence" (IET 2026-01-31 [04:27]–[04:38]). Mark "sellers/buyers induced" at the swept level and pre-draw the trap zone where they will re-enter (IET 2026-04-21 [02:22]–[02:42]; IET 2026-03-03 [06:32] — copy-paste the annotation each repeat).

### P3 — Buildup / liquidity ladder ("build, build, build")

- **Looks like:** a stair-step or channel of repeated cycles — high taken, low respecting low (bullish version) — or a tight multi-hour range pressing against a level; "you can draw a trend line, whatever you want to do... we are building at the lows" (IET 2026-02-24 "$6,600 UJ" [04:03]).
- **Means:** each cycle prints one more confirmed pool; total resting liquidity beyond the pressed level grows. "Look at the build-up we had just below it. It's beautiful. This is what we like to see" (IET 2026-03-17 "$10k in 4 min" [04:04]–[04:24]); "for around roughly an hour and a half... we simply build in this channel, chop around... nonetheless, we are building liquidity down here" (IET 2026-02-17 "Training The Eyes 13" [01:26]–[01:47]).
- **Confirmed by:** duration and repetition — 3-day buildups on the hourly (IET 2026-04-14 [05:14]–[05:34]), 20-hour boxes (file 03 §7), 5-day accumulations consumed in 18 hours (IET 2026-06-09 [12:06]).
- **Invalidated / not it when:** the "ladder" has no left-hand references (nothing respected) — then it is unreadable chop, P10.
- **Action:** trail attention to the *extreme-most* unswept rung; that is the level whose sweep arms the entry (file 02 §E; "we are building a tremendous amount of liquidity here at 5173... we want to wait for the sellers to get taken out", IET 2026-03-17 [02:44]–[03:04]).

### P4 — Liquidity block (LB)

- **Looks like:** the swing left behind where a prior sweep terminated — a wick/swing that itself took out a level and reversed, often containing the sweep candle's imbalance. Drawn as a box: "I grabbed this last low, dragged it over to cover the bottom of the liquidity block" (IET 2026-07-21 [02:26]–[02:46]); "I grab the last low... and I grab the whole swing low, the whole liquidity block" (IET 2026-02-10 "Simple Strategy" [06:47]–[07:07]).
- **Means:** no resting orders beyond it — "since we have swept out previous liquidity, now we view this high to hold no liquidity, meaning we don't have a reason to run above it" (IET 2026-03-10 "Liquidity Blocks" [01:52]–[02:12]).
- **Confirmed by:** the sweep that created it (must have actually traded through a real level, P1 — "you need to pair it with liquidity", IET 2026-03-10 [04:32]–[04:52]).
- **Invalidated / not it when:** price later *respects* it and moves away — it re-acquires liquidity (file 01 §5b); or it was never a sweep of anything ("all you're going to be doing is plotting on highs and lows that have been taken out, taking entries left, right, and center", IET 2026-03-10 [04:12]).
- **Action:** stop-loss anchor and entry zone — "all the liquidity block does for you essentially is provide you a level to place your stop loss... no liquidity block, no stop loss... we need an LB to take an entry" (IET 2026-03-10 [04:52]–[05:12]); hard gate: "no liquidity block, no entry" (IET 2026-07-14 [07:55]–[08:15]). Expect deep stabs into it before the move (IET 2026-02-24 [05:24]–[06:04]). Not every LB is traded — untraded ones still mark expected-reaction spots (IET 2026-03-10 [05:32]–[06:12]).

### P5 — Engineered liquidity (the respecting swing in front of the target)

- **Looks like:** after the left-side sweep, price approaches the target pool, prints a swing that respects it (undershoot allowed), and retraces. "We tapped into this red box and moved away — this is now known as engineered liquidity" (IET 2026-02-24 [02:02]–[02:42]); "both these highs would be considered engineered liquidity" (IET 2026-04-14 [03:53]–[04:13]).
- **Means:** counter-trend participants entered in front of your target; their stops refresh the target pool and give price its reason to go there.
- **Confirmed by:** the respect-and-move-away against the *target* level specifically (file 03 §1.3).
- **Invalidated / not it when:** price trades straight through the target (target consumed — bias re-evaluation, P8).
- **Action:** arming condition for the setup; later becomes a partial/target on the way out — "all of this buyer liquidity that was engineered on the way up, you want to use that as your target" (IET 2026-05-05 "Stacking Entries" [07:53]).

### P6 — Trap sequence (induce → sweep → reverse; the tradeable event)

- **Looks like:** the completed grammar: P2 induces early traders → P3 builds their pool → a fast counter-leg stabs the level they live behind, into/near a P4 LB — often a wick or one-two violent candles — then rejection. "Momentum to the downside tells us anybody selling at these highs — trap" (IET 2026-03-03 "All You Need" [05:49]–[06:10]); "we spike out a level of liquidity... price now comes up and takes out the high. We trap the sellers" (IET 2026-07-14 [15:36]–[15:56]).
- **Means:** the early traders' stops are consumed; one side of the book is cleared; the confirmed pool on the opposite side is the remaining magnet.
- **Confirmed by:** all preconditions pre-existing (target intact, LB present, early traders identified). "We must only trade once the early traders are trapped. No ifs, ands, or buts" (IET 2026-06-09 [09:05]).
- **Invalidated / not it when:** no one was induced on the swept side (plain sweep — no trade); no target pool beyond (no reason to move); no LB (no stop → no entry, even if everything else is right, IET 2026-07-14 [07:35]–[08:15]).
- **Action:** enter at the stab (market or pre-set limit in the LB/imbalance), stop beyond the LB, target the opposing pool(s). Entry can feel like "catching a falling knife. However, it is per the system and it is extremely repeatable" (IET 2026-07-21 [14:09]–[14:29]).

### P7 — The 4-candle sniper entry (candle-anatomy version of P6)

The whole model compressed into adjacent-candle anatomy (all cites IET 2026-03-24 "Sniper Entries"; works "on specific time frames... it's fractal, it happens everywhere" [01:02]–[01:22]):

- **Looks like (bullish):** Candle 1 sets a low → Candle 2 spikes below C1's low and "rapidly closes back up above the previous candle, leaving this massive wick to the downside" — that wick is the LTF liquidity block ([01:42]–[02:02]) → Candle 3 prints a (higher) low and closes with the trend ([02:02]–[02:22]) → **Candle 4 spikes below Candle 3's low** → "as soon as price spikes below candle three low... you want to start looking for longs in this area" ([02:22]–[02:42]).
- **Means:** C2's wick proved no liquidity below (LB); C3's low collected fresh buyers; C4's spike traps them directly above a no-liquidity zone.
- **Confirmed by:** C4's stab holding *above* the C2-wick LB — "the wick could come all the way down here if it wants... everything is valid above this low" ([02:42], [04:02]–[04:22]).
- **Invalidated / not it when:** C4 only *respects* C3's low without trading below — "are we buying that? We're not buying that. We need price to trade below this low" ([05:02]–[05:22]); or price closes through the C2 LB (stop).
- **Action:** buy the C3-low break, stop below the C2-wick LB ("stop loss below the low" [03:02]–[03:22]); bearish is the stated mirror (candle-2 high LB, sell the candle-3-high break, "stop loss above the liquidity block" [08:22]–[08:42]). Observed instances: daily ([03:42]–[04:42]), 4H ([04:42]–[05:42]), 15m ([06:02]–[07:22]), plus the sell case ([07:42]–[09:23]). This same anatomy underlies the PDL/prev-30m-low entries of file 09 §3.4.
