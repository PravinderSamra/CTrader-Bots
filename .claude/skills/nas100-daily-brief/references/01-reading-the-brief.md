# 01 — Reading the brief

Six sections. What each is for, and what to do with it.

## Header
Day, session window, and the relationship to the last scan.
- **"PREP scan"** = non-trading day. Numbers are last session's close. Do not
  present them as live.
- **"New trading day"** = everything is fresh; yesterday's extremes are now the
  prior-day levels.
- **"Continuation"** = lead with what changed, don't re-read the whole thing.

## 1. The call
Direction, score, conviction, and the strategy. `Driven by:` names the
components that produced it; the full 24-check table is collapsed underneath.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ≥ +6 / ≤ −6 | A real call. Trade it |
| ±3 to ±5 | Mild lean. Half size, or wait for confluence |
| −2 to +2 | **No edge. Standing aside is the correct answer** |

## 2. Regime
Each block gives the technical read first, then what it means. Three parts:
**gamma** (who's on the other side), **volatility** (how much movement is
priced), **rates/FX** (the macro pull). Then **Shape of the day** — how the
expiry buckets combine (see reference 02). Then the FRED block: slower-moving
context that sets the week's mood, not an intraday trigger.

## 3. Fuel & range budget
How much of a normal day's range is already spent, and what's left. This drives
stop management — see reference 03.

## 4. Level board
The only levels to mark. Merged by price: a level named twice (⭐) has two
independent reasons behind it and is the strongest on the board.
- `(stretch)` = beyond the range budget. Partials only.
- The footnote list is context. **Do not mark it.**

Levels the board *doesn't* show are deliberate: single-touch swing extremes
(noise) and negative gamma shelves (price accelerates through them, so there is
nothing to trade there).

## 5. Events
Scheduled risk. A High-impact print inside 90 minutes is a hard gate.

## 6. News
Split in two. **Auto-scored** items are unambiguous and already in the bias
number. **Needs judgement** items were deliberately not scored — keyword logic
gets negation, modals and relevance wrong. Those are for you to read.
Expect 0–3 auto-scored on a normal day; 0 on a quiet weekend is correct.
